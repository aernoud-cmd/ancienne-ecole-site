Depuis 1899

From village schoolhouse to family retreat

Local farmers pooled their own money to build this Catholic girls’ school around 1900, at the highest point of Troche — designed by the same architects behind the nearby Chartreuse du Glandier. Two nuns ran it for decades; parents paid in farm produce, not francs.

The last schoolmistress lived out her days here until 1999. We found the house standing empty and spent years, from 2003 on, bringing it back to life — today it welcomes 20–25 families and groups of friends a year, in a corner of France that still feels like a “milieu de nulle part.”
